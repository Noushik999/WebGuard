5. Methodology

WebGuard performed a passive, non-destructive assessment: it fetched the target's public pages exactly as a normal browser would and analyzed response headers, TLS behavior, cookies, CORS responses, and visible technology signals. No exploitation, authentication bypass, credential use, or denial-of-service testing was performed. Each scanner module runs in isolation with timeouts and request budgets; a module failure never aborts the assessment.

12. Limitations

  • Passive assessment only: no authenticated scanning, deep crawling, or active exploitation.
  • The score reflects only WebGuard's checks; absence of findings is not proof of security.
  • Version-based dependency notes are risk signals — vulnerabilities are not confirmed by version alone.
  • AI-generated explanations interpret scanner evidence and never create findings.
